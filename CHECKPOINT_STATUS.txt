KNOWN ISSUE
-----------
The tokenizer that was used to train my_minigpt_v2.pth (vocab=129) no longer
exists on disk. It was saved inside the Colab runtime and was not downloaded
before the session ended. The 12 missing token IDs (117-128) exist in the
model weights but cannot be produced by the current tokenizer.

Padding the tokenizer with 12 arbitrary tokens would match the vocab size
number but would NOT restore the original token-to-ID mapping. That approach
is rejected.

       checkpoints/my_minigpt_fineweb.pth  (new, will prompt before overwrite)
